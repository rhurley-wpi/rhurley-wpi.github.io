About Me

I am a third-year PhD candidate in Civil, Environmental, and Architectural Engineering at Worcester Polytechnic Institute (WPI), where I am also affiliated with the Department of Fire Protection Engineering. Advised by Professor Shichao Liu, my research investigates the intersection of indoor air quality, wildfire smoke transport, and occupant health.


My work seeks to enhance resilience against both indoor and outdoor pollutants. By investigating both laboratory-scale experiments and behavioral psychology models, I aim to understand how the built environment, material properties and human decision-making prolong or mitigate smoke exposure.


Before my doctoral studies, I conducted research at Lafayette College and at WPI investigating the cognitive impacts of CO2 exposure. My goal is to transition into a post-doctoral or faculty position in related fields including environmental science and engineering.
