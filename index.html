About Me

I am a PhD student and researcher at WPI focusing on environmental health and indoor air quality. My work specifically examines the behavior of wildfire-sourced volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and how they penetrate and persist in common indoor materials.


Through my research, I aim to bridge the gap between engineering and social science to better protect public health during environmental disasters.
