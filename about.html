About Me

I am a second-year PhD candidate in Civil, Environmental and Architectural Engineering at Worcester Polytechnic Institute, advised by Professor Shichao Liu. My research focuses on indoor air quality, wildfire smoke exposure and occupant health and behavior. I am interested in predicting how material properties and porosity can determine indoor pollutant transport. I am eager to learn more about community-engaged research practices, developing smoke or natural disaster readiness plans.

My current projects examine re-emission of wildfire-sourced pollutants from building materials. My future work aims to focus on how prescribed burning practices influence indoor smoke transport of SVOCs and understanding elderly populations smoke perceptions and mitigation behaviors. Previously, I have investigated the impacts of elevated carbon dioxide exposure due to mask-wearing on student cognition during the COVID-19 pandemic. As an undergraduate student at Lafayette College, my research focused on predicting flash flooding behaviors in the mid-Atlantic region.
