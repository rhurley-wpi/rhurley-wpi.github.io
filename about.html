About

I am a third-year PhD candidate in Civil, Environmental and Architectural Engineering and affiliated with the Department of Fire Protection Engineering at Worcester Polytechnic Institute. I am advised by Professor Shichao Liu. My research focuses on indoor air quality, wildfire smoke exposure and occupant decision-making and health. I am interested in understanding how the built environment, human decision-making, and indoor material properties influence and prolong exposure to wildfire smoke. I am eager to learn more about community-engaged research practices, developing smoke or natural disaster readiness plans, and the efficacy of indoor smoke mitigation techniques.

I enjoy continuing to learn and engage with different academic fields. In my undergradute research, I had investigated the impacts of elevated carbon dioxide exposure due to mask-wearing on student cognition during the COVID-19 pandemic. As a student at Lafayette College, my research focused on predicting flash flooding behaviors in the mid-Atlantic region. My future goal is to transition into a post-doctoral, faculty, or government position and my interests are numerous span several related fields, including environmental science, chemistry, environmental policy, geography and civil, mechanical, or environmental engineering.
